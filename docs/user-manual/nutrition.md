# Nutrition

## Choose Your Nutrition Lens

Open `Nutrition` and choose the amount of detail that is practical right now:

- `Full macros` shows calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fat.
- `Calories + protein` hides the other macro detail.
- `Meal check-in` replaces numbers with one neutral choice per meal.
- `Off` removes nutrition from Today while keeping all existing data.

Changing the lens never deletes foods, saved meals, goals, diary history, or
meal check-ins. Existing installations start with full macros.

For either numeric lens, choose 5%, 10%, or 15% target flexibility. Keepfit
shows each saved goal as a lower-to-upper range rather than an exact pass/fail
number.

## Add Foods

Use the `Library` area to create foods you regularly eat.

Each food stores:

- food name;
- serving label and serving amount;
- calories;
- protein;
- carbohydrates;
- fat.

Foods can also be favorited or archived.

## Saved Meals

Saved meals let you reuse a group of foods together.

Good examples:

- a standard breakfast;
- a post-workout shake;
- a lunch you repeat often.

When you log a saved meal, Keepfit expands it into diary entries for that day.

## Logging the Day

Typical daily use:

1. Move to the date you want.
2. Open a meal section.
3. Add a food or a saved meal.
4. Choose a 0.5x, 1x, 1.5x, or 2x serving preset, or enter another amount.
5. Repeat for the rest of the day.

## Totals and Goals

The summary card shows only the metrics selected by the current lens and
compares them with target ranges based on goals from `Settings`.

If yesterday's matching meal exists, use `Repeat yesterday` inside that meal
section. It replaces only that meal. Later edits to a saved meal do not change
the diary entries already created from it.

Use `Replace day with yesterday` when the whole day repeats. This replaces all
entries on the selected day.

## Meal Check-ins

Meal check-in mode offers three deliberately neutral choices:

- `Balanced`: protein and produce were both present.
- `One focus`: either protein or produce was present.
- `Flexible`: something else, or you are unsure.

From Today, tap `Check in`, then choose an option for the meal. Selecting a
different option updates that meal instead of creating a duplicate. Weekly
review reports check-in consistency without scoring the day.

## Good Practices

- Keep serving labels simple and realistic.
- Archive foods you no longer use instead of deleting your working history.
- Use saved meals for common combinations to reduce logging time.
