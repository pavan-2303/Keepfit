# Weekly Review

Keepfit turns recent local records into a short weekly check-in. It works
offline and does not call the optional assistant.

## Open the review

On Today, find **Weekly review** and choose **Review last week**. Keepfit reviews
the most recently completed Monday through Sunday, so the current unfinished
week does not count against the summary.

The seven-day ribbon uses:

- a check for a completed planned workout;
- a move icon for a workout moved to another date;
- a dot for a planned workout without a completed session; and
- a dash for a rest day.

Full, shortened, and minimum sessions all count as completed. The review may
also show protein-log consistency and step averages when those records are
available. Health Connect permission is never requested just because the
review opened.

## Understand the suggestion

Keepfit can show up to two deterministic drafts:

- make one workout a minimum session;
- shorten one workout;
- move one workout to a free day; or
- add one set to the first exercise, capped at six sets.

Each draft states the current workout, the proposed change, and the local
evidence behind it. The wording recognizes completed work and returns after an
interruption; there is no lost-streak penalty or score.

Choose a draft label to compare suggestions. Choose **Edit day** to select a
different eligible day. Nothing is saved while browsing or editing.

## Apply or dismiss

Choose **Apply for next week** to create one dated adjustment in the coming
Monday-Sunday. This does not edit the recurring weekly plan, reusable workout
template, completed history, or any later week.

Choose **Dismiss this review** to record the decision without changing any
workout. Keepfit will wait for the next completed week before offering another
decision.

## Pause reviews

Choose **Pause weekly reviews** when a check-in is not useful. Workout,
nutrition, progress, and step tracking continue normally. The Today card lets
you resume reviews at any time.
