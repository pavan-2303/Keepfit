## Summary

Move legal attribution out of the exercise-reading path, preserve it in one
packaged and in-app location, and rebuild exercise details around movement
guidance rather than catalogue provenance.

## Design Direction

Keep the v0.19.0 field-guide palette and offline-safe sans typography. Use a
near-full-height movement sheet, one compact fact band, genuinely sequential
step markers, and a stable bottom action row. Avoid decorative chip grids and
repeated cards.
