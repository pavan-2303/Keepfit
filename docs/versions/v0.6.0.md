## Sprint Goal

Make nutrition useful at the level of detail the user can sustain, with faster
reuse and no loss of existing history when that level changes.

## Delivered Increment

The user can choose detailed macros, calorie-and-protein focus, neutral
meal-quality check-ins, or disabled nutrition. Today and the weekly review show
only the selected level, while favorites, recents, serving presets, saved
meals, previous meals, and previous days reduce repeated entry work.

## Behavioral Contract

- The four persisted modes are `DETAILED_MACROS`, `CALORIES_PROTEIN`,
  `MEAL_QUALITY`, and `DISABLED`.
- Existing and restored settings without a mode default to detailed macros so
  the current experience does not disappear after upgrade.
- Existing calorie and macro goals remain the midpoint of a display range. A
  local flexibility preference of 5%, 10%, or 15% controls the lower and upper
  bounds and defaults to 10%.
- Detailed mode shows calorie, protein, carbohydrate, and fat ranges.
  Calorie-and-protein mode shows only calorie and protein ranges.
- Meal-quality mode stores at most one check-in per local date and meal type.
  Its neutral choices are balanced, one focus, and flexible; changing a choice
  replaces that meal's check-in without adding duplicates.
- Disabled mode removes nutrition prompts and totals from Today and contributes
  no nutrition signal to weekly review. The Nutrition destination still offers
  a clear way to choose another mode.
- Food diary entries, saved meals, foods, goals, and meal-quality history are
  never deleted when the mode changes.
- A serving preset changes only the pending amount. Adding a food still
  requires an explicit selection.
- Repeating yesterday's meal replaces only the selected target meal after an
  explicit action. Duplicate-yesterday retains its existing whole-day replace
  behavior.
- Food rows expanded from a saved meal or copied from another day are
  independent entries. Later saved-meal item changes do not change prior diary
  entries.
- Today offers a mode-specific summary and a direct action into Nutrition. In
  meal-quality mode, a check-in can be completed in two deliberate actions
  from Today: open the check-in and choose a quality.
- Weekly review uses calorie/protein logging consistency for the two numeric
  modes, meal-quality check-in consistency for meal-quality mode, and no
  nutrition evidence when disabled.
- All behavior remains local and works without an account, network, catalogue,
  or AI service.

## Work Items

### Item 1 - Persist depth, ranges, and meal-quality check-ins

**Why**

Mode changes must survive relaunch and backup while keeping structured daily
check-ins queryable and independent from diary history.

**What to do**

1. Add depth and target-flexibility preferences to DataStore and backups.
2. Add the meal-quality entity, DAO operations, schema migration, exported
   schema, and backup summary coverage.
3. Preserve backward compatibility for old settings and databases.

**Affected files or subsystems**

- `core:preferences`
- `core:database`
- `feature:settings` backup

**Dependencies**

- Existing Room, DataStore, and encrypted-backup infrastructure

**Estimated effort**

- 2d

**Done when**

- [ ] Mode, flexibility, and check-ins persist with compatible defaults.
- [ ] Migration and backup round trips preserve every new value.

### Item 2 - Add deterministic summaries and safe reuse commands

**Why**

Every mode needs one explainable summary, and repeated meals should take less
effort without creating hidden relationships or accidental duplicates.

**What to do**

1. Add target-range and depth-specific summary rules with unit tests.
2. Add previous-meal availability and a transactional selected-meal repeat.
3. Add meal-quality observe/upsert commands and mode-aware weekly evidence.

**Affected files or subsystems**

- `feature:nutrition`
- `feature:review`
- nutrition DAO

**Dependencies**

- Item 1

**Estimated effort**

- 3d

**Done when**

- [ ] Summaries expose only the selected depth and bounded target ranges.
- [ ] Meal repeat and saved-meal expansion remain independent and deterministic.

### Item 3 - Build a calmer mode-specific nutrition experience

**Why**

A sustainable mode is only useful if irrelevant controls disappear and common
actions remain close to the current day and meal.

**What to do**

1. Add the nutrition lens selector and range flexibility control.
2. Render mode-specific Today, summary, diary, quality-check-in, and disabled
   states.
3. Add serving presets, favorites/recents-first entry, and repeat-meal actions.

**Affected files or subsystems**

- Nutrition Compose UI and ViewModel
- Today composition

**Dependencies**

- Items 1-2

**Estimated effort**

- 3d

**Done when**

- [ ] All four modes are understandable and switch without data loss.
- [ ] Meal-quality check-in takes two actions from Today.
- [ ] Numeric modes provide faster food and meal reuse.
