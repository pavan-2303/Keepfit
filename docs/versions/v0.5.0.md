> **Linked initiative:** [Practical Fitness Journey and Optional AI Coach](../backlog/items/practical-fitness-journey-and-ai-coach.md)
> **Status:** In Progress
> **Estimated effort:** 8-12d
> **Risk:** High
> **Prerequisites:** v0.4.0 Low-Friction Workout Execution, dated workout
> occurrences, workout feedback, nutrition totals, and optional Health Connect
> steps

---

## Sprint Goal

Turn recent local records into a calm two-minute weekly review and let the user
approve, edit, or dismiss one small adjustment for the coming week without AI
or an automatic plan rewrite.

## Delivered Increment

From Today, a user can open the most recently completed Monday-Sunday review,
see planned and completed workouts, available performance, nutrition, and step
signals, receive evidence-based encouragement, and inspect at most two bounded
drafts. Applying a draft creates only a dated occurrence for the following
week. The user can change the affected day, dismiss the review, or pause and
later resume weekly reviews.

## Frozen Review Contract

- The review window is the most recently completed ISO week, Monday through
  Sunday. The comparison window is the seven dates immediately before it.
- A workout counts as completed only when its session has `completedAt`.
  Full, shortened, and minimum sessions all count as completed.
- Planned counts resolve the active recurring schedule and any dated occurrence
  override for each date. A skipped occurrence is not counted as completed.
- Performance improvement requires a higher completed-set weight or repetition
  result for the same exercise than in the comparison window.
- A return is recognized when the review week has a completion, the comparison
  week has none, and at least one completion exists in the preceding 14 dates.
- Nutrition reports only days with diary entries and rounded averages. Steps
  appear only when Health Connect is available and permission is already
  granted; opening a review never requests permission.
- Draft priority is recovery, schedule, then volume. No more than two drafts are
  shown. Low completion or difficult/low-energy feedback favors a minimum or
  shortened session; a missed planned day may offer a move to a free day;
  strong completion plus measured improvement may offer one extra set, capped
  at six.
- Previewing or editing a draft never writes. Approval snapshots the selected
  workout as one dated occurrence in the coming Monday-Sunday week and records
  the decision. Dismissal records no plan mutation.
- Motivation cites a local fact, recognizes partial completion and returns, and
  never uses streak loss, failure, guilt, coins, or leaderboards.

## Scope Boundaries

- Keep calculation, copy, persistence, and application entirely offline and
  deterministic.
- Reuse dated occurrences so one reviewed week cannot rewrite templates,
  recurring plans, completed sessions, or later weeks.
- Store structured review outcomes in Room and the pause toggle in DataStore.
- Do not add weekly-review notifications, monthly recaps, recovery surveys,
  nutrition-depth controls, body-weight recommendations, AI explanations, or
  automatic application.

## Work Items

### Item 1 - Persist review decisions safely

**Why**

Keepfit must remember that a week was reviewed without treating draft previews
as durable fitness data.

**What to do**

1. Add a weekly review outcome entity keyed by reviewed week start.
2. Add DAO reads and transactional apply/dismiss commands that link an approved
   decision to its dated workout occurrence.
3. Add the weekly-review pause preference.
4. Increment Room from schema 8 to 9, export the schema, and cover migration and
   encrypted backup restore.

**Affected files or subsystems**

- `core:database`, `core:preferences`, and `feature:settings` backup verification

**Dependencies**

- Existing workout occurrence and settings storage

**Estimated effort**

- 2-3d

**Done when**

- [ ] Schema-8 data migrates intact and schema 9 preserves review outcomes
  through encrypted backup restore.
- [ ] Preview and edit paths perform no database mutation.
- [ ] Approve and dismiss are idempotent for one reviewed week.
- [ ] Pause and resume survive process recreation without hiding other Today
  content.

### Item 2 - Calculate local review evidence and drafts

**Why**

The review must be useful and explainable even when Health Connect or AI is not
available.

**What to do**

1. Build pure window, completion, improvement, interruption, return, nutrition,
   and optional-step calculations.
2. Select at most two drafts using the frozen priority and bounds.
3. Produce explicit evidence, current state, proposed state, and reason text.
4. Expose insufficient-data, ready, completed, and paused states through a
   state-driven repository and ViewModel.

**Affected files or subsystems**

- New `feature:review` module and app-level optional Health Connect adapter

**Dependencies**

- Item 1 and existing workout/nutrition queries

**Estimated effort**

- 3d

**Done when**

- [ ] Trend windows and return recognition are deterministic at date boundaries.
- [ ] Missing nutrition or step data omits that signal without blocking review.
- [ ] Draft selection never exceeds two and every draft explains its evidence.
- [ ] Re-reading an already decided week returns the recorded outcome rather
  than another prompt.

### Item 3 - Build the two-minute review flow

**Why**

The user needs a focused decision, not another dense dashboard.

**What to do**

1. Add a Today entry card and a dedicated review route.
2. Use a seven-day week ribbon to show planned, completed, missed, moved, and
   rest dates at a glance.
3. Present achievements and available trends in plain, non-judgmental language.
4. Show one selected draft with approve, edit-day, choose-another, and dismiss
   actions, plus pause/resume controls and accessible states.

**Affected files or subsystems**

- `feature:review` Compose UI and ViewModel; `app` navigation and Today

**Dependencies**

- Items 1-2

**Estimated effort**

- 3d

**Done when**

- [ ] The normal path can be understood and decided without visiting another
  feature screen.
- [ ] Approve shows the exact affected date and workload before writing.
- [ ] Edit changes only the dated draft and never a reusable plan.
- [ ] Ready, insufficient-data, completed, dismissed, paused, and error states
  are usable with large text and screen-reader labels.

### Item 4 - Integrate, document, and verify

**Why**

Review decisions touch planning, backup, optional steps, and Today, so they need
release-level validation.

**What to do**

1. Update the application to version `0.5.0` and version code `5`.
2. Update current architecture, data model, README, and user guidance.
3. Run focused red-green tests, broad unit tests, lint, build, migration,
   backup, repository, Compose, and manual Android checks.

**Affected files or subsystems**

- Application metadata, documentation lifecycle, and verification fixtures

**Dependencies**

- Items 1-3

**Estimated effort**

- 1-3d

**Done when**

- [ ] The app reports `0.5.0`, builds, and opens existing local data intact.
- [ ] Current documentation matches only the delivered review behavior.
- [ ] Required automated and manual verification is recorded before a done
  record is created.

## Testing Requirements

- [ ] Unit tests cover ISO windows, partial completion, improvements,
  interruptions, returns, nutrition/step availability, copy, and draft bounds.
- [ ] DAO and repository tests cover approve, edited approval, dismiss,
  idempotency, and proof that preview does not mutate plans or occurrences.
- [ ] Room migration 8-to-9 and encrypted backup restore preserve existing and
  new records.
- [ ] Compose tests cover approve, edit, dismiss, insufficient-data, completed,
  and paused/resumed states.
- [ ] `testDebugUnitTest`, `lintDebug`, `assembleDebug`, and relevant connected
  suites pass on an available Android emulator or device.

## Definition of Done

- [ ] Every work-item checklist is complete.
- [ ] The delivered increment is usable, not placeholder-only.
- [ ] Required tests and verification steps pass.
- [ ] Drafts never mutate data before approval and approved changes affect only
  the selected coming-week occurrence.
- [ ] Weekly review remains useful with AI disabled and Health Connect absent.
- [ ] Follow-up work is captured separately rather than left implicit.
